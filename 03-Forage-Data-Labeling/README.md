# Forage Data Labeling Job Simulation

## About this project

Completed the Forage Academy Data Labeling Job Simulation through [Forage](https://www.theforage.com/simulations/forage/data-labeling-c1t0).

This virtual job simulation provided practical experience in data annotation, intent classification, sentiment analysis, personally identifiable information (PII) detection, and quality assurance.

The simulation involved working with fictional customer-support messages, applying consistent labeling guidelines, identifying sensitive information, reviewing existing annotations, and recommending improvements to data-labeling processes.

## Tasks completed

### Task 1: Batch Labeling & PII Awareness

- Analysed fictional customer-support messages.
- Classified messages according to their intent.
- Identified the sentiment expressed in customer messages.
- Detected and flagged personally identifiable information (PII).
- Applied consistent annotation guidelines across multiple messages.
- Evaluated ambiguous cases and documented the reasoning behind labeling decisions.
- Practised privacy-aware data handling.

**Deliverable:** Completed message-labeling work and PII identification.

### Task 2: Review, Quality Control & Iteration

- Reviewed previously labeled customer-support messages.
- Evaluated the accuracy and consistency of existing annotations.
- Applied Keep, Fix, or Flag decisions during quality review.
- Identified incorrect, inconsistent, or potentially problematic labels.
- Provided brief explanations to justify review decisions.
- Suggested targeted improvements to labeling guidelines.
- Focused on improving annotation consistency, data quality, and privacy awareness.

**Deliverable:** Quality-review results and labeling guideline improvement recommendations.

## Tools and techniques used

- Data Annotation – Applying structured labels to customer-support messages.
- Intent Classification – Identifying the purpose of customer messages.
- Sentiment Analysis – Categorizing customer sentiment.
- PII Detection – Identifying sensitive personal information.
- Quality Assurance – Reviewing and validating annotations.
- Documentation – Recording decisions and improvement suggestions.
