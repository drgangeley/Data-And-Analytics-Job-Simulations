# Tata Data Visualisation: Empowering Business with Effective Insights

## About this project

Completed the Tata Group Data Visualisation Job Simulation through [Forage](https://www.theforage.com/simulations/tata/data-visualisation-p5xo).

This virtual job simulation, offered in association with Tata Insights and Quants (Tata iQ), provided practical experience in business data analysis, data cleaning, visualization, and data storytelling.

The simulation involved analysing a fictional online retail business dataset and developing meaningful insights to support strategic business decisions by senior management, particularly the CEO and CMO.

## Tasks completed

### Task 1: Framing the Business Scenario

- Analysed the business requirements from the perspectives of the CEO and CMO.
- Prepared eight relevant business questions, four for each executive.
- Identified important business metrics and analytical requirements.
- Considered how data analysis could support revenue growth, customer understanding, and expansion strategies.

**Deliverable:** CEO and CMO business questions.

### Task 2: Choosing the Right Visuals

- Explored different data visualization techniques.
- Identified appropriate chart types for different business questions.
- Considered the importance of clarity, readability, and effective data storytelling.
- Selected visual formats to communicate business insights effectively to senior management.

**Deliverable:** Visualization selection and supporting work.

### Task 3: Creating Effective Visuals

- Cleaned the online retail dataset before performing analysis.
- Identified invalid records, including negative quantities and negative unit prices.
- Applied appropriate data-cleaning and validation techniques.
- Created data visualizations using [Tableau / Power BI].
- Analysed monthly revenue trends for 2011.
- Identified the top 10 revenue-generating countries, excluding the United Kingdom.
- Analysed the top 10 customers by revenue.
- Visualized geographical product demand to identify potential expansion opportunities.

**Deliverable:** Interactive dashboards and supporting analysis.

### Task 4: Communicating Insights and Analysis

- Prepared a presentation explaining the analytical process.
- Explained data-cleaning methods and visualization choices.
- Presented findings from the four business scenarios.
- Communicated important insights and recommendations to the CEO and CMO.
- Prepared and submitted a presentation video explaining the analysis and its business implications.

**Deliverable:** Presentation script, slides .Added the files in the main folder.
